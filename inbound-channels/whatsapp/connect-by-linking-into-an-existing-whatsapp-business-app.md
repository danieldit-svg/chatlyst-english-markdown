# Connect by Linking into an Existing Whatsapp Business App

### Activate the widget

After you create a widget, the next page opens automatically so you can review the widget details.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (235).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Connect by Linking into an Existing Whatsapp Business App

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Select **Connect with WhatsApp.**

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment77632f7a b93b 4f09 8879 f72a30e51465image.png" alt=""></div>

In the Meta window, log in to your Facebook account (if required). When prompted, connect with Effex, then select **Continue**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Meta may take a few minutes to load this window. Please be patient.
{% endhint %}

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachmentd8f5a984 4974 445b 9abf ef0bde628014image (1).png" alt="" width="375"></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Select a **Business Portfolio.**

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachmentb19144b8 0e4c 4577 9808 a9ae9477a6ceimage (1).png" alt="" width="375"></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Select **Connect a WhatsApp Business app**, then select **Next**

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment281be94f d6dd 4f0d aefe 2390cc77b29eimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

2. If prompted, complete the business profile (required only the first time):
   * Select your **Country**.
   * Enter your **Website**.
   * Select **Next** to proceed.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The website must be valid. This example uses Google for demonstration purposes.
{% endhint %}

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment3b809554 392b 4c4a a316 4975bf9fdb12Screenshot_2026 06 01_at_9.57.03_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

3. In WhatsApp Business phone number, enter the Phone Number registered to your WhatsApp Business account, then select **Next**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment9780b3a5 9b60 43ad 85ec 41b7514347c8Screenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.01.11_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Review what is shared with Effex

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment5ce90d9e 14a8 4119 a3ee 47997b7934dbScreenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.02.03_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
For the WhatsApp channel, incoming messages in the Chat section will display the user’s name and phone number.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (245).png>)
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Link the WhatsApp Business app (QR code)

1. Open WhatsApp Business on your phone and scan the QR code.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment1a76958b 8807 4bd2 aea0 80049a98ff42Screenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.05.10_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

2. Open **WA Business** on your phone.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment2c3aa2a7 29a8 4fca 873e cf8bff8760fcimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

3. Go to **Settings**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment5d552174 02a5 4f48 ab21 84bb6c1d034cimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

4. Go to **Account**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment678052be d4df 4f8c a743 192d52a07157image.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

5. Select **Business Platform**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachmentb1cb2b7a 3840 4b43 b207 256985bc2622image.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

6. Select **Connect to the Business Platform**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment4a45237c f450 49ec 94f1 20c77a2e8cdeimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

4. Select **Share all chats** or **Don’t share chats**, then select **Confirm**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment124f0435 84c3 4f13 868d b9ac6f65ee0cimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

5. Scan the QR code on your computer screen. This step may take up to 45 seconds.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment871f6ae9 1838 440e 95ca 463b3126ed3fimage.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

6. After the previous step is complete, you will see the following on both devices:

* On your computer:

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment6408feb9 0508 4702 89c5 5cee8e841fb9Screenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.20.27_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

* On your phone:

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment343cd538 9159 4252 9fcc 9f6c2fe65559image.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

7. Continue setup on your computer, select the **Time zone** for this WhatsApp Business account.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment7ec9c3ce 8252 4673 b19e 24f91590a530Screenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.22.02_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

8. Review the details you shared with Chatlyst, then select **Confirm**. Meta will connect the account.

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachmentf5788d08 80ce 4b81 b889 8ff4f35c0f9cScreenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.22.48_PM (1).png" alt="" width="375"></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
When your account connects successfully, you will see a confirmation message on your computer and phone.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you are unable to connect, please contact hello@chatlyst.io
{% endhint %}

* On your computer:

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachment23ae4d82 d250 4b1e a557 fd33ee149aceScreenshot_2026 06 01_at_10.24.21_PM.png" alt="" width="375"></div>

* On your phone:

<div data-with-frame="true"><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/attachmentc65ddbbb 07a8 4a35 b453 04bbf4f9f3d1image.png" alt="" width="375"></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
The widget status in Chatlyst changes from **Draft** to **Active**.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (239).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
