### Shortcuts

<details>

<summary><strong>Company Name</strong> </summary>

Company Name shows the current company name in the sidebar. Select it to return to the Dashboard and reset navigation to the main overview.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Notifications</strong> </summary>

Notifications shows recent updates. Select the ring bell icon to open the panel. Review notifications one at a time and clear them individually, or select **Mark all as read**.

<div align="center"><img src="../.gitbook/assets/attachment39412c91 77f1 40fa a658 c2d020d3a094Screenshot_2026 04 14_at_4.31.23_PM (1).png" alt="" width="375"></div>

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Minimise Sidebar</strong></summary>

Minimise Sidebar gives the main content area more room. Move your cursor to the sidebar edge to minimise or reopen it.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Menu Section</strong></summary>

Menu Section provides quick access to the main areas of Chatlyst.

* [Dashboard](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/dashboard)
* [Chat](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/chat)
* [Customers](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/customers)
* [Business Knowledge](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/business-knowledge)
* [Train & Test bot](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/train-and-test-bot)
* [Inbound Channel](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/inbound-channels)

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Usage Details</strong></summary>

Usage Details shows current credit usage, including credits used and remaining. It also includes shortcuts to [top up](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/top-up-guideline) credits and leave feedback.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Help Center</strong></summary>

[Help Center](https://chatlyst.gitbook.io/chatlyst-user-guide) is your go-to place for answers, guides, and support resources. It helps you solve common issues quickly and directs you to the right next step when you need extra help.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Setup Tour</strong></summary>

Setup Tour gives you a quick overview of the key controls. It helps you get familiar with the layout, so you can start using the product faster.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>User name and email</strong></summary>

User name and email shows the current account details. Use this to confirm the correct profile is in use.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Settings</strong></summary>

**Settings** opens the [Settings](https://app.gitbook.com/s/BW7n4ClAhZvbgvusylCd/settings) page, where you can update account and workspace preferences.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Logout</strong></summary>

**Logout** signs out of the current account and returns to the [log in](log-in.md) screen.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Chatlyst Support</strong></summary>

When a user signs in to Chatlyst, the **Chatlyst Support** chat bubble appears in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (581).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* Users can open the bubble to ask for help with any part of the Chatlyst app.
* If the chat bubble or conversation window blocks something on the screen, users can **drag it to another position** to improve visibility.

</details>
