## Functions

This feature requires additional setup before it can be enabled and shown on the Dashboard. Select Contact Effex, then contact [hello@chatlyst.io](mailto:hello@chatlyst.io) for details.

### Tutorial

### Upload Additional Document

Upload additional documents in multiple ways: Core Template section: Select **Upload Additional Document** in the top-right corner, or select **Upload** at the bottom. Follow more on [Additional Documents](additional-documents.md).

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (154).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
View Additional Document — Select **View All** at the bottom of the page to open the [Additional Documents](additional-documents.md) section.
