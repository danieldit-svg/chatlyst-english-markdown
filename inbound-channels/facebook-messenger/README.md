## Important Notes:

#### **Prerequisite for Connection**

Before connecting a Facebook Page to Chatlyst, one specific requirement must be completed within the Meta platform.

* **Add to Meta Business Suite:** The Facebook Page must be added to an active Meta Business Suite account.

#### Facebook 3-Way Synchronisation

If you registered using the Facebook, you will retain full access to the application. Messages are synchronized across both Chatlyst (Chat section) and the Facebook Messenger.

#### **Message History**

Chatlyst does not sync historical messages from the Facebook Messenger. Only messages within active Chatlyst sessions will be visible in the platform.

## Manage Widgets

### **Widget On/Off**

Use the switch in the top-right corner to turn a channel on or off.

When a channel is turned off, it is disabled immediately, and the chatbot stops appearing on that channel.

Any widgets connected to the channel automatically change from **Active** to **Draft**. This removes them from the live experience until the channel is enabled again.

### **Recent Metrics**

The tab shows the number of conversations and messages from the past 30 days. Use these metrics to confirm the channel is receiving traffic and validate performance before making configuration updates.
